More Than a Place.
A Way of Life.

From a warm cup of Arabic coffee to the rhythm of the dabke, discover the traditions, celebrations and everyday gestures that make Jordan feel like home.

ROOTED IN HISTORY · ALIVE TODAY

The soul of Jordan lives in its people.

Jordanian culture is shaped by generations of hospitality, faith, family and creativity. From Bedouin traditions and village gatherings to the celebrations of modern cities, every custom tells a story of belonging.

Explore eight expressions of our living heritage — and the small details that turn a visit into a connection.

01 / 08 — OUR FAITH

Faith, family & festive moments.

Across Jordan, religious occasions are a time for reflection, generosity, togetherness and traditions shared between generations.

THE RHYTHM OF DAILY LIFE

Prayer & Spiritual Life

The call to prayer, Friday gatherings and community worship are familiar parts of daily life for many Jordanians.

THE HOLY MONTH

Ramadan & Iftar

As the sun sets, families and neighbours gather to break their fast and share meals, visits and acts of kindness.

TIME TOGETHER

Eid Celebrations

Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha bring family visits, festive food, gifts and joyful gatherings.

A country of many traditions. Jordan's Christian communities also celebrate Christmas, Easter and other religious occasions, adding to the country's rich cultural tapestry.

02 / 08 — SOCIAL TRADITIONS

A welcome you can feel.

Hospitality is more than a custom in Jordan — it is a way of honouring others. Whether in a bustling home or a quiet desert camp, guests are often greeted with warmth, conversation and something to share.

01

Arabic Coffee

Served in small cups, often with dates. Traditionally, gently shaking the cup signals that you have had enough.

02

Bedouin Tents

The beit al-sha'ar, or house of hair, reflects traditions of shelter, storytelling and welcoming travellers.

03

Family & Community

Visiting relatives, gathering with neighbours and supporting one another remain important parts of social life.

A CUP OF COFFEE, A STORY TO SHARE
03 / 08 — LIFE'S CELEBRATIONS

Where every wedding tells a story.

Jordanian weddings blend music, family and regional customs in celebrations full of energy and meaning.

LOVE · FAMILY · COMMUNITY

Moments worth celebrating together.

01

The Jahah

A traditional family gathering in which representatives formally ask for a couple's engagement or marriage.

02

Henna Night

In some families and communities, a pre-wedding celebration with music, henna and loved ones.

03

Zaffeh & Dabke

Celebratory processions, lively songs and group dancing fill the occasion with joy.

THREADS OF IDENTITY · STORIES IN EVERY STITCH
04 / 08 — TRADITIONAL CLOTHING

Heritage, woven by hand.

Traditional clothing carries memories of place, family and craftsmanship. Across Jordan, embroidery patterns, fabrics and styles vary from one community to another.

✦

Embroidered Dresses

Detailed stitching, colours and motifs reflect regional styles and generations of skilled work.

✦

Shemagh & Agal

Headwear that remains a recognisable expression of Jordanian and broader Arab heritage.

✦

The Bisht & Traditional Robes

Garments often associated with formal occasions, celebrations and expressions of respect.

05 / 08 — MUSIC & MOVEMENT

The rhythm of our roots.

Music and dance bring people together, carrying stories across the desert, the villages and the cities of Jordan.

SONGS THAT CARRY HISTORY
STEPS WE SHARE
01

Dabke

A spirited group dance often performed at weddings and celebrations.

02

Al-Samer

Traditional communal performance combining poetry, singing and movement.

03

Rababa & Desert Songs

Melodies and verse associated with storytelling and Bedouin musical traditions.

06 / 08 — HANDMADE HERITAGE

Made by hand. Remembered forever.

Behind each mosaic, woven textile and handmade object is a craftsperson preserving traditions through patience and imagination.

MADABA & BEYOND

The Art of Mosaics

Stone by stone, artisans keep a centuries-old visual tradition alive.

SKILLED HANDS

Handmade Crafts

Pottery, painting and other crafts reflect the creativity of local makers.

COLOURS OF THE PAST

Stories in Stone

Patterns and symbols connect today's visitors with Jordan's layered history.

One flag. Many shared moments.

On national occasions, streets and public squares come alive with flags, music and a shared sense of pride.

08 / 08 — AROUND THE TABLE

Pull up a seat. You're welcome here.

Food is a language of welcome in Jordan. Sharing a meal is often as much about conversation, connection and generosity as it is about what is on the plate.

01

Mansaf & Shared Meals

Jordan's iconic dish is associated with hospitality and important gatherings, often served for people to share.

02

Tea, Coffee & Conversation

A visit often begins with an invitation to sit, relax and enjoy a warm drink.

03

Food for Every Occasion

From Eid sweets to family feasts, food brings people together through the seasons.
