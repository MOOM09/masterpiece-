Welcome to Jordan

Jordan is full of beautiful places and friendly people. Visit Petra, enjoy the Red Sea in Aqaba, and explore our cities, mountains and deserts. There is so much to see and do here.

Discover Jordan’s history

Jordan has many stories to tell. You can visit ancient cities, old castles and famous places like Petra. Our green hills and wide deserts are also part of what makes Jordan special.

Our traditions

In Jordan, we love to welcome guests. Sharing Arabic coffee, spending time with family and making art are important parts of our culture.

Taste Jordanian food

Try mansaf, one of Jordan’s most loved dishes. It is made with rice, meat and a special yogurt sauce. We love to share good food with family and friends.
